## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Application Technology at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences ([CASIA](http://english.ia.cas.cn/)) and the School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences ([UCAS](https://english.ucas.ac.cn/)), supervised by Prof. [Dongbin Zhao](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~zhaodongbin) and Assoc. Prof. [Yuanheng Zhu](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~zhuyuanheng).
Before joining CASIA, I received my B.E. degree with honors from the School of Automation Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China ([UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/)).

My research interests lie in **Reinforcement Learning**, **Multi-Agent Cooperation**, and **Robotics**. I aim to enable autonomous agents to understand open worlds, cooperate with each other, and interact with humans.
