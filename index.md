## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student (expected to graduate in **2027**) at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences ([CASIA](http://english.ia.cas.cn/)) and the School of Artificial Intelligence, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences ([UCAS](https://english.ucas.ac.cn/)), supervised by Prof. [Dongbin Zhao](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~zhaodongbin) and Assoc. Prof. [Yuanheng Zhu](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~zhuyuanheng). Prior to my Ph.D. studies, I received my **B.E. degree with honors (0.7%)** from the School of Automation Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China ([UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/)). My research focuses on **Reinforcement Learning**, **World Model**, and **Robot Learning**, with a focus on bridging **learning, planning, and control** to enable robust and scalable real-world robot intelligence.



我是赵子杰，一名就读于 **[中国科学院自动化研究所](http://www.ia.cas.cn/)** 的博士生，预计于 **2027 年毕业**，导师为 **[赵冬斌](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~zhaodongbin)** 研究员和 **[朱圆恒](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~zhuyuanheng)** 副研究员。在攻读博士学位之前，我于 **[电子科技大学](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/)** 自动化工程学院获得**工学荣誉学士学位（0.7%）**。我的研究方向主要包括 **强化学习**、**世界模型** 和 **机器人学习**, 重点关注融合 **学习、规划与控制** 的方法，以实现鲁棒且可扩展的真实世界机器人智能。
