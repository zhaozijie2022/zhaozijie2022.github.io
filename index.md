## About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences ([CASIA](http://english.ia.cas.cn/)), expected to graduate in **June 2027**. 
I am advised by Prof. [Dongbin Zhao](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~zhaodongbin) and Prof. [Yuanheng Zhu](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~zhuyuanheng). 
Before my Ph.D., I received my **Honours Degree (Top 0.7%)** from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China ([UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/)). My research focuses on **Reinforcement Learning**, **World Model**, and **Multi-Modal Learning**.
I am currently seeking full-time opportunities for 2027, particularly in embodied AI, robotics, reinforcement learning, and world models. Please feel free to reach out if you are interested in potential opportunities.

目前正在寻找 2027 届全职工作机会，主要关注具身智能、世界模型和多模态大模型等方向，欢迎联系。
